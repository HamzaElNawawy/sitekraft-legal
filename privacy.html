SiteKraft Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

Information processed

SiteKraft processes account authorization tokens, basic connected-profile information, source-video metadata, local media files, transcripts, clip plans, and publishing results needed to run the workflow.

How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate the operator, prepare clips, upload approved media, check publishing status, prevent duplicate jobs, and recover interrupted work.

Storage and sharing

Processing and workflow state are stored on the operator's computer. Information is sent to YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, or another destination only when required to perform an operator-authorized action. SiteKraft does not sell personal information.

Retention and deletion

Local credentials and job data remain until the operator removes them. Platform access can be revoked through the connected platform, and local SiteKraft credentials and generated files can be deleted by the operator.

Security

SiteKraft stores credentials outside exported n8n workflows and limits its worker service to a private local Docker network. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Children

SiteKraft is not directed to children and is intended for use by an authorized account owner.

Changes

This policy may be updated when SiteKraft's processing or platform integrations change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
